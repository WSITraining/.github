
# Wellcome Sanger Institute - Scientific Training and Events

> We fund, develop and deliver training courses, conferences and other events (in-person and online) focusing on genomics in research and healthcare

### Our vision and shared purpose

To amplify scientific excellence, enabling scientists to deliver the Wellcome Sanger Institute’s mission and accelerate real-world impact.

### Our mission and role

We design and deliver world-class training, conferences, and workshops that strengthen scientific capability and broaden access to the Sanger Institute’s knowledge and tools.

From local skills workshops to global genomics training across **Asia, Africa, and Latin America**, we extend the Institute’s reach and expertise worldwide.

### What you will find here

This organisation hosts resources supporting scientific training and events, including:

- 📚 Course materials and practical exercises
- 🧬 Training workflows and analysis resources
- 💻 Informatics setup guides
- 🛠️ Workshop templates and supporting documentation
- 🌍 Open resources for genomics education and skills development

Our work supports **researchers, trainers, educators, and scientific communities** at different stages of their genomics journey.
