### Course repository structure
Each course will have a dedicated **course landing page** containing general information about the course, including its aims, learning objectives, target audience, course structure, and other relevant details.

The course repository will be organised into folders containing the resources used to support course delivery. These may include:

-  **Presentation slides** and other teaching materials.
-  **Bioinformatics resources**, including code, workflows, and practical exercises.
-  **Course datasets** and other data required for exercises and analysis.
-  **Additional resources**, documentation, and supporting information that may be useful to participants and educators.

The repository is intended to provide a structured and accessible collection of materials associated with the course.
